# Smaller Right-Aligned Secondary Recolha

The secondary `A seguir` recolha remains an undecorated typographic block, but becomes about 15–20% smaller and aligns to the far right of its available space. The primary recolha keeps its existing scale and hierarchy.

At the 961×541 target, the secondary column narrows from 9rem to 7.5rem. The block uses `justify-self: end`, right-aligned text, and reduced time, client, location, and plate sizes. No data or component behavior changes.
